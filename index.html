    Chrome turns your voice into text using its own speech service, so it needs an internet connection. This tool never records or saves audio and never listens to the interviewer. Wear headphones so the interviewer's voice does not reach your microphone.

    Setup check
    1. Mic test (8 seconds)
    Sit where you will sit for the interview, then press Start mic test.
    2. Say a sentence
    Say: “Good morning, I am ready for my interview.”

    When the level says Good and your sentence appears, you are ready. Close this panel, then press Start.

    3. Test with a recording (silent)
    Speed

    Nothing plays out loud and nothing leaves this laptop. It tests answer splits, the timer, the circle colours and the black screen. Words, pace and fillers stay empty, because Chrome only turns a live microphone into words. Your saved mic level is not changed.
